Joe

Kim Hyun-joong (born June 6, 1986) is a prominent South Korean actor, singer, and songwriter, famous as the leader of K-pop group SS501 and for iconic roles in dramas like Boys Over Flowers (Yoon Ji-hoo) and Playful Kiss, solidifying his status as a major Hallyu star, known for both his musical success (with solo hits like "Heat") and charismatic acting, though his career has also seen personal controversies. He's known for his musical talent, stage presence, and roles that showcase depth, evolving from idol to solo artist and actor with releases and tours in Asia and beyond.
